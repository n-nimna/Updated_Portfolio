Put the photos of the people who sent you testimonials in THIS folder
(for example:  kasun.jpg   ruchira.png).

Then in  src/data/siteData.js  find TESTIMONIALS and add the file name:

  { quote: "...", name: "Kasun Perera", role: "Lecturer, NSBM", photo: "kasun.jpg" },

Tips: use a square photo (face in the middle), at least 200 x 200 px. JPG or PNG.
If you leave  photo  out, the person's initials are shown instead.
